How to read it

  • Sign convention: counter-clockwise (↺) is positive, clockwise (↻) is negative.
  • Pick P as the pivot to predict the direction. The floor contact point is the instantaneous axis of rotation for rolling. Friction and the normal force pass through it, so the string torque alone decides the turn.
  • Pick C as the pivot to see friction work. The string torque and the friction torque both act about the center, and Newton's second law for the center of mass adds the force balance.
  • Critical angle: with the string off the bottom of the axle, the string line passes through P when cos θ = r / R. Flatter than that, the spool rolls toward the hand. Steeper, it rolls away.
  • Model: a 1 kg spool, massless string that stays tangent to the axle, flat floor, rolling friction ignored. Kinetic friction is 0.8 of the static value. The run stops after 3 s of simulated time.
